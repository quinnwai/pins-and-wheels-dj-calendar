Pins & Wheels DJ Nights

The guest DJ lineup for Adult Skate Night at Pins & Wheels in Austin, as a calendar you can subscribe to. It updates itself every day from their events page.

Add to Apple Calendar Add to Google Calendar
  • iPhone, iPad, Mac: tap Add to Apple Calendar, then Subscribe.
  • Google Calendar: open the Google link on a computer and confirm. It then shows up in the Google Calendar app on Android and iPhone too.
  • Anything else: copy the feed URL and add it as a subscribed or "from URL" calendar.
  • Google refreshes subscribed calendars on its own schedule, usually within a day. Apple checks more often.
